KALALUKA HEALTH POST STAFF HOUSE NEARS
COMPLETION
T
he construction of a semi-
detached staff house at
Kalaluka Health Post under
the 2025 Constituency Development
Fund (CDF) is nearing completion,
with works currently standing at
approximately 80 percent.
The project, valued at K1.8 million,
encompasses not only the
construction of the staff house but
also the installation of a water
reticulation system and the
construction of an incinerator.
Kalaluka health post staff house front view
These additional components are
critical in ensuring the facility
operates efficiently while upholding
proper sanitation and healthcare
waste management standards.
Once completed, the staff house is
expected to significantly improve
accommodation for health
personnel, thereby enhancing
service delivery at the facility.
It is worth noting that Kalaluka
Health Post itself is a 2022 CDF
project which is yet to be
commissioned, as it awaits full
Side view of the staff house operational readiness.
The commissioning of the health
post will be done upon completion of
the staff house, water reticulation
system, and incinerator.
This integrated approach
underscores the commitment to
ensuring that health facilities are not
only constructed but are fully
equipped and supported with the
necessary infrastructure to provide
quality healthcare services to the
community.

T
he Department of Maritime
and Inland Waterways
(DMIW), under the Ministry of
Transport and Logistics, has
commenced safety sensitization on the
Zambezi River. In line with Zambia's
decentralization policy, certain
maritime functions such as pontoon
services and harbor management have
been devolved to Local Authorities.
This initiative aims to enhance safety
standards and reduce risks for
operators and passengers navigating
the Zambezi River.
Recently, Harbour Master Mr. Martin
Mr Martin Lubinda (in a reflector) sensitising the staff for African Queen
Lubinda, now stationed in Livingstone and David Livingstone Safari Lodge
under Livingstone City Council, held
sensitization meetings with The
African Queen Company and David
Livingstone Safari Lodge & Spa. The
sessions focused on safe navigation on
the Zambezi River, covering:
- Safety requirements on board
- Common risks on the Zambezi River
- Emergency preparedness
- Safe navigation practices
Mr. Lubinda was accompanied by a
Council firefighter, a marine officer
from the Zambia Police, and the
Council's Public Relations Officer.
